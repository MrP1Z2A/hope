Payment

  • Packages are paid monthly in advance to Hope Education.
  • Lessons are scheduled once payment is received.
  • To stop, tell us before the next month’s payment.

Student ID

  • You get a Student ID after your first payment is verified.
  • It is a six-digit number, for example 100001.
  • You keep the same ID for every subject, tutor and renewal. It is never given to anyone else.
  • It is used for attendance, progress, payments and support. It is not a password.

Attendance

Attendance rate

  • Attend every scheduled lesson on time and stay for the full lesson.
  • Keep a monthly attendance rate of at least 80%.
  • Attendance rate = scheduled lessons attended ÷ total scheduled lessons × 100. Example: 7 of 8 lessons = 87.5%.
  • Rescheduled lessons are recorded separately and do not remove the original absence.

Absences

  • Approved absence: tell your tutor at least 24 hours before the lesson and complete the Hope Education Absence Form. Maximum 2 per month.
  • Unapproved absence: a lesson missed without 24 hours’ notice and the Absence Form.
  • Rescheduling always depends on tutor availability.

Emergencies

  • Illness, family emergencies and serious internet or power problems can happen. If you cannot give 24 hours’ notice, contact your tutor as soon as possible.
  • Hope Education may make reasonable exceptions after reviewing the situation.

Low attendance

  • Below 75% in a month: the student receives an attendance warning and should reach 80% the next month.
  • Still below 80% the month after a warning: Hope Education may inform a parent or guardian. For students under 18, we may contact them earlier if concerns are serious or repeated.
  • Below 50% for two months in a row: Hope Education may stop the tutoring service, after reviewing the attendance record and any exceptional circumstances.
  • We may also review a student’s lesson schedule if absences keep repeating, even with advance notice.

These rules encourage steady learning and fair use of tutors’ time. They are not meant to punish students facing genuine emergencies.

Safeguarding

  • Tutors are friendly but always professional.
  • Tutors only contact students through Hope Education, never personal social media.
  • Parents of students under 18 are kept informed and may watch any lesson.

Lessons

  • Join from a quiet place with stable internet.
  • Lessons are only recorded if everyone agrees.

Privacy

  • We only collect what we need to run lessons.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Ask us any time to see or delete it.
